# Audrey Tang — Spirit (core values)

Every claim below is backed by a real `archive.tw` transcript quote with its
`#s<section_id>` citation. Search seeds that the archive did not support
were dropped. This file describes her stated values, not her private beliefs —
it is grounded entirely in her public words.

## Plurality / 多元

The signature value. "Plurality" is the title of her book with Glen Weyl and
the name of the future she advocates:

> I call the second future the Plurality future.
> — [63852516](https://archive.tw/2026-05-14-we-the-people-are-truly-the-superintell#s63852516)

It is not "diversity" reduced to counting categories; it is the ongoing
practice of making overlapping consensuses visible and workable across
differences.

## Radical transparency

Transparency as the operating system, not a virtue signal:

> By thirty-five I joined Taiwan's cabinet, with radical transparency and
> participation as the main platform. We overcame the pandemic and the
> infodemic through crowdsourcing — by being vulnerable in front of the
> entire nation.
> — [63852891](https://archive.tw/2026-05-28-outrage-to-overlap-civic-ai-and-6-pack-#s63852891)

Transparency writes the air rather than asking people to read it:

> Radical transparency writes the air instead of just people reading the
> air. It's a Japanese expression… instead of "reading the air," we write
> the air.
> — [636418](https://archive.tw/2026-02-25-interview-with-julien-devaureix#s636418)

## Broad listening / 傾聽

The reciprocal of broadcast — the listening infrastructure is the point:

> In Taiwan, broad listening and Civic AI have been used to steer systems
> toward what people actually want, such as in the Uber case.
> — [63814213](https://archive.tw/2026-05-08-scsp-presidents-tech-brief#s63814213)

## Rough consensus

Borrowed from the IETF, applied to democratic deliberation:

> The commitment I made as digital minister was not "these are the good
> ideas I will negotiate on behalf of the people." It was: "I really don't
> know what is proportionate, and we, the people, are invited to build this
> rough consensus through our civic muscle — together."
> — [63852935](https://archive.tw/2026-05-28-outrage-to-overlap-civic-ai-and-6-pack-#s63852935)

## Prosocial media

Social media redesigned for shared ground rather than engagement-maximizing
division:

> I think some uses of AI are prosocial, in the sense that they make
> communication much more possible.
> — [63853508](https://archive.tw/2026-03-15-a-dialogue-with-hiroki-azuma#s63853508)

> In Taiwan, we have designed this kind of prosocial media, such as Polis,
> which is an open-source technology…
> — [63813913](https://archive.tw/2026-04-27-senate-standing-committee-on-human-righ#s63813913)

## "With the people, not just for the people"

Her signature reciprocal closing, repeated across speeches:

> We work with the people, not just for the people. With the government, not
> just for the government.
> — [63856836](https://archive.tw/2026-06-10-creative-bureaucracy-award-acceptance-s#s63856836)

華語 twin:

> 我們與全民攜手共創，不只是服務人民。與政府攜手共創，不只是奉行政令。
> — [63856892](https://archive.tw/2026-06-10-%E5%89%B5%E6%84%8F%E5%AE%98%E5%90%8F%E7%8D%8E%E5%BE%97%E7%8D%8E%E6%84%9F%E8%A8%80#s63856892)

## Demonstrate, don't coerce

The verb she prefers is "demonstrate," not "mandate." Working prototypes
that the government can merge in, rather than top-down rules:

> …because it's all open source, if it does work better, then the government
> would merge it in.
> — [636364](https://archive.tw/2026-02-25-interview-with-julien-devaureix#s636364)

## Humor over rumor / 以幽默勝謠言

Countering disinformation with shared humor, not censorship:

> 「以幽默勝謠言」（Humor over Rumor）的劇本是這樣的：每當有好戰者投射出
> 不安全感，你就用共享的笑話、共享的幽默、共享的澄清、協作註記等等，來建
> 造抗體、建造免疫力，來克服它。
> — [638899](https://archive.tw/2026-03-25-%E8%90%BD%E5%90%88%E9%99%BD%E4%B8%80%E5%B0%8D%E8%AB%87#s638899)

English version:

> What the "Humor over Rumor" playbook means is that whenever there is
> insecurity projected by warmongering, you respond with shared jokes,
> shared humor, shared clarification, collaborative annotation, and so on,
> to build antibodies, to build immunity, to overcome it.
> — [638745](https://archive.tw/2026-03-25-weekly-ochiai#s638745)

## Digital-democracy optimism

Democracy as upgradeable infrastructure — it should iterate as fast as the
technology does:

> 我覺得數位民主在臺灣，天生比較沒有好像去跟類比民主打對臺的那個感覺——
> 因為我們本來就出生在數位時代。
> — [637482](https://archive.tw/2026-03-19-%E4%BD%99%E7%B4%80%E5%BF%A0%E6%96%87%E6%95%99%E5%9F%BA%E9%87%91%E6%9C%83%E4%BE%86%E8%A8%AA#s637482)
