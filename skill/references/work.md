# Audrey Tang — Work (topical scope)

Each topic is grounded in a cited `archive.tw` transcript. When a question
falls within one of these, use the cited section as the entry point; when it
falls outside, say so plainly rather than stretching the analogy.

## g0v / 零時政府

The civic-tech shadow-government movement that she helped seed — "g0v" is
"gov" with the `o` replaced by a zero:

> Registered this website, g0v.tw, and the government services, which is
> something that g-o-v.tw, if you change an o to a zero, you get into the
> shadow government made by civic technologies that is always open source
> and works sometimes better. And because it's all open source, if it does
> work better, then the government would merge it in.
> — [636364](https://archive.tw/2026-02-25-interview-with-julien-devaureix#s636364)

## Join / join.gov.tw (Public Policy Participation Platform)

The national e-petition platform — 5,000 co-signers triggers a binding
official response:

> …the national platform of participation, join.gov.tw, anybody can collect
> 5,000 signatures and force a national-level policy discussion, and the
> corresponding ministry must respond.
> — [627337](https://archive.tw/2025-11-03-oxford-union-talk#s627337)

華語 version:

> 政府很快將其制度化，納入「公共政策網路參與平臺」——並精心將其命名為
> Join.gov.tw，以表明其功能。在 Join 平臺上，任何獲得 5,000 人連署的
> 提案，相關部會就必須回應。
> — [624782](https://archive.tw/2025-10-15-%E6%97%A5%E6%9C%AC%E5%9C%8B%E6%9C%83ai-%E8%88%87%E6%B0%91%E4%B8%BB%E8%B7%9F%E9%BB%A8%E6%B4%BE%E8%AD%B0%E5%93%A1%E6%9C%83%E8%AD%B0#s624782)

## Presidential Hackathon

Annual award that turns cross-sector prototypes into policy:

> That intergenerational energy helped grow the Presidential Hackathon. Here
> is how it works: local teams in a district or municipality take problems
> surfaced on Join, build a working prototype, and then every year we award
> five teams a trophy, it is a projector! If you turn it on, it projects the
> president giving you the trophy. Very meta.
> — [630602](https://archive.tw/2026-01-17-from-taiwan-to-germany-how-radical-part#s630602)

## Mask-availability map & pandemic response

The pandemic-era infrastructure that made real-time pharmacy mask stock
visible — explicitly a protocol, not a single platform:

> There are more than a hundred mask map visualizations. And so it's not a
> single platform. It is rather a protocol, a way for platforms to talk to
> each other, the way for pharmacies to report their real-time stock
> inventory of masks available.
> — [636370](https://archive.tw/2026-02-25-interview-with-julien-devaureix#s636370)

## The *Plurality* book

Co-authored with Glen Weyl, Tenzin, and others. It inspired real political
action — including Anno Takahiro's run for the Tokyo governorship as a
VTuber:

> A couple of years ago Anno-san read the Plurality book that I wrote
> together with Glen Weyl, Tenzin and many others, and decided to act on it
> in Japanese politics. He called me via video and said, "Nobody knows me,
> nobody under the age of forty has ever become governor of Tokyo, and I
> don't have party support."
> — [63852938](https://archive.tw/2026-05-28-outrage-to-overlap-civic-ai-and-6-pack-#s63852938)

## AI governance / Alignment Assemblies

The Taiwan-prototype mechanism for aligning AI with contested social
values — deliberative, not technocratic:

> In Taiwan, that infrastructure was prototyped a couple of years ago as what
> we call Alignment Assemblies. It is a mechanism that takes the discipline
> of repair — not just in living rooms, not just in individual families —
> and scales it to the entire population.
> — [63852923](https://archive.tw/2026-05-28-outrage-to-overlap-civic-ai-and-6-pack-#s63852923)

華語 version:

> 在台灣，這套基礎設施在幾年前以我們所稱的「對齊大會」（Alignment
> Assemblies）做了原型。它是一套機制，把修復的紀律——不只是在客廳
> 裡、不只是在個別家庭裡——擴展到全體人民。
> — [63852797](https://archive.tw/2026-05-28-%E5%BE%9E%E6%86%A4%E6%84%9B%E5%88%B0%E4%BA%A4%E9%9B%86-%E4%BB%81%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E6%85%A7%E8%88%87%E9%97%9C%E6%87%B7%E5%85%AD%E5%8A%9B#s63852797)
